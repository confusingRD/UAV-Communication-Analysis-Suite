## 2026-11-17 — Phase 2A bounded spherical ray BVP (**Reconstructed**)

- **Phase:** Phase 2A
- **Objective:** Implement and numerically verify the initial bounded, nonducting spherical endpoint BVP for UAV-to-ground propagation while preserving the CubeSat baseline.
- **Implementation summary:** Added immutable ray contracts and `trace_spherical_between`; supported short-arc radial-monotonic and single-minimum rays for strictly increasing `q=n*r`; added a UAV adapter, deterministic demonstration/export, ray visualization, and ten verification tests. Physical ray tracing remained separate from geometric/EER references.
- **Scientific traceability:** The Phase 2A report records Bouguer invariant `b=n(r)r cos(theta)`, the radial quadratures for angle, geometric length, and refractivity integral, the `r=r_min+u^2` tangent regularization, physical-Earth coordinates, and the scalar horizontally stratified assumptions. The declared local central-angle bound is below 0.35 rad; default endpoint targets are `1e-10 rad` and `0.1 m` where applicable.
- **Historically reported commands:** `.venv-science\Scripts\python.exe -m pytest -q`; `.venv-science\Scripts\python.exe -m uav_cas.phase2a_demo --output-dir outputs\phase2a`.
- **Historically reported results:** Pre-change baseline: 64 passed plus 15 passed subtests in 8.45 s. Final original CubeSat suite: 38 passed plus 15 passed subtests in 7.10 s. New Phase 2A suite: 10 passed in 1.99 s. Combined suite: 74 passed plus 15 passed subtests in 10.22 s. The 130 km demonstration reported 0.002409 m Cartesian endpoint error, `3.73984e-9` maximum reconstructed invariant relative error, and 6.868749 m minimum smooth-surface clearance. These are report-derived numerical/software verification results, not results rerun during history initialization and not experimental validation.
- **Bugs/numerical issues reported:** Tangent integrals have a square-root singularity; unsupported nonmonotonic `q`, exhausted root work, endpoint residual failure, insufficient profile bounds, and ground grazing/intersection require distinct outcomes.
- **Solutions/workarounds reported:** Regularized the tangent using `r=r_min+u^2`; split quadrature at profile knots; retained explicit status/reason fields; returned numerical failure instead of a nearby path; never used EER as fallback.
- **Remaining limitations/next steps:** No ducts, multipath, long paths, terrain, diffraction, reflection, ray-tube focusing, absorption generator, measured-atmosphere validation, coverage search, GUI, or executable. Broader branch analysis and near-singular convergence work remain before coverage.
- **Evidence:** `docs/PROGRESS_PHASE2A.md`, `outputs/phase2a/phase2a_summary.json`, `tests/test_ray_bvp.py`, and `tests/test_phase2a_demo.py`.
